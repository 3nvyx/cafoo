# One group period per month, and account codes that name one company

The group closes one month at a time: there is one Period per month for every Subsidiary, and the CFO locks the group's month, not each company's. Per-company locks would let one company's month stay open while the consolidated Board Pack treats it as final, and the demo does not need them yet, so they stay on the roadmap. Account codes stay unique across the group: the root company keeps bare codes (`1000`) and every other company adds its own code (`1000-UK`, `1900-ELIM`), so the group code (the part before the dash) lines accounts up for consolidation without a mapping table. Because a code now belongs to exactly one company, the module refuses a journal line on another company's account, in Proposals, postings and seed entries alike, and a bank match pays out of the bank line's own company's cash, receivable and payable accounts.
