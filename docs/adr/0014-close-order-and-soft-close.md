# Months close in order, a group month locks only once consolidated, and opening a close is a soft close

A month-end close used to accept any order. A later month could start its close or be locked while an earlier one was still open. A group month could lock with a Revaluation or Elimination that no longer matched the books. Three rules now fix the order, and one pure file (`packages/ledger-core/src/close-order.ts`, with no imports) states them for the ledger module, the agents and Demo mode, so all three refuse the same things in the same words. First, months close in order: a month's close starts, the Advisor asks for its lock, and the CFO locks it only once every earlier month is locked. A month waiting for its lock (`closing`) still counts as not locked. The module refuses a lock request or a lock out of order. It also refuses the first move of a close task out of todo, because that move opens the close. The agent gateway answers a start out of order with 409 and the plain reason. Second, opening a close is a soft close: from the Advisor's opening onward the live feed and the continuous Bookkeeper stop booking into the month, which leaves the close's own Proposals and the CFO's corrections. The module enforces this for the feed. The continuous Bookkeeper stops on the agents' side, because the module cannot tell its Proposals from the close's. The marker is the one the module already used for the feed: a close task of the month has left todo. The live close now moves the tasks out of todo when it opens, as the scripted close always did. The Period's status keeps its meaning: `open` while the agents work, `closing` once the Advisor has requested the lock, `closed` once the CFO locks it. We did not make the soft close a status of its own, because `closing` already means "the lock was requested" to the screens, the lock gate and the agents. Demo mode replays a close recorded for September 2026 only and refuses every other month instead of posting September's entries into it. It has no feed, so nothing posts outside the close there. Third, a group month locks only once consolidated. A group month has more than one trading company and an elimination company. The module refuses the Advisor's lock request and the CFO's lock of such a month while either of two things holds. The first is a foreign-currency balance that is not at the month's closing rate, or that has no closing rate recorded, at a trading company that has an FX gain or loss account. The second is an intercompany pair that is not eliminated: the elimination company does not carry exactly what the reporting-currency source companies show. The module recomputes both from the posted ledger with the same arithmetic as its revaluation and elimination checks, over the same companies and pairs that the Consolidation agent consolidates. The refusal (`consolidationDueRefusal` in `close-order.ts`) names each balance and pair still standing. Because the check runs at the lock, it also catches a correction posted after the consolidation, such as a CFO reversal on an intercompany account. A moved revaluation is proposed again for the difference. A stale elimination is reversed and proposed again, because the module refuses a second elimination of a month that already carries one. A month with nothing to consolidate, and a month that is not a group month, locks as before. A correction posted after the lock goes to the next open month, whose own close consolidates it. A balance elimination reverses the next month, so it posts only while that month is in the books and open. When it is not, the refusal says to add or reopen that month first. The seed's closed months now carry their balance eliminations, each reversed the next month, so a closed month that is reopened and left unchanged locks again. The seed cannot link a reversal to its elimination the way an applied Proposal does, so after a correction to a reopened seeded month, the CFO reverses the seeded reversal by hand along with the elimination. Three gaps remain. First, consolidation can go stale after a later CFO decision. The closes consolidate during the run, as before, while company-level Proposals may still be waiting for the CFO, so a decision confirmed afterwards that moves a foreign-currency or intercompany balance leaves the Revaluation or Elimination stale. We considered making consolidation wait until every other Proposal of the month is decided, but then a close in which nobody has confirmed yet files no consolidation at all, which the demo and the charter evals rely on. The lock catches the stale figures instead: the refusal names each balance and pair to redo, and a person or the next close run redoes them. Second, once a company has been revalued for the month, the refusal reports any of its balances off the closing rate as moved since the revaluation, even one the revaluation did not cover. Third, the check leaves out an account that holds the company's own currency as well as a foreign one, such as a euro receipt into a dollar bank account, unless the own-currency lines are revaluation adjustments. The revaluation, in the module and in ledger-core, restates a whole account, so it cannot restate the foreign part of such an account. The Consolidation agent does not handle these accounts either.
