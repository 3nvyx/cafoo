# Unmatched bank exceptions require separate CFO acceptance before locking

Rejecting an uncertain bank match leaves a receipt unresolved, so it cannot establish that reconciliation is complete.
The CFO may still close the Period after separately accepting the exact remaining Bank Exceptions with a reason.
That acceptance is immutable audit evidence, bound to the reviewed bank rows and the recorded bank and ledger revision.
Changed evidence requires fresh acceptance, and agents cannot accept on the CFO's behalf.
Accepted exceptions stay unmatched and their reconciliation task stays incomplete after locking, so a closed Period does not falsely claim that every receipt was reconciled.
