# Revaluations and eliminations are Proposals; translation is computed, never posted

Period-end revaluation and intercompany eliminations change balances, so they go through the same gate as every other change: a Consolidation worker proposes them, the Red Team critiques, the Advisor rules, and at or above the Materiality threshold the CFO confirms. The module recomputes every figure from the posted ledger and the recorded rates when the Proposal is made, when it is ruled on and when it posts, and refuses one that is off by a cent, so the amounts come from the books rather than from an agent. Eliminations post to a dedicated Elimination subsidiary so no trading company's books carry consolidation entries, and a balance elimination reverses itself the next month, like an accrual, because the next close eliminates the balance as it then stands. Translation into the Reporting currency is computed when a report is drawn (assets and liabilities at the closing rate, income and expense at each month's average rate, equity at its posting month's average) and the CTA is the figure that makes that translation balance, never a posted entry, so a corrected rate needs no reversal. A Proposal's amount is always in the Reporting currency, converted at the month's closing rate, so one Materiality threshold covers the whole group; the module and the ledger engine both round conversions half away from zero, so they agree to the cent.
