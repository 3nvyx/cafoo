# Policy: group consolidation, revaluation and eliminations
Cafoo Inc reports the group in US dollars; Cafoo Ltd keeps its books in pounds sterling. At each month end a company restates every asset and liability it holds in another currency at that month's recorded closing rate, and books the change to its own FX gain or loss account in its own currency; income and expense lines are never restated. Intercompany balances (amounts due between the companies and the intercompany loan) are eliminated from the combined balance sheet at the source company's own balance through the month, posted only to the elimination subsidiary and reversed on the first day of the next month. Intercompany service fees are eliminated from the combined income statement at the month's activity and never reversed. The other company's side, translated at the closing rate (balances) or the month's average rate (activity), is shown next to each elimination; a difference over one dollar is raised in the close thread for a person to resolve and is never plugged. Translation of each company's books into dollars uses the current-rate method: assets and liabilities at the closing rate, revenue and expense at each month's average rate, equity at the rate of its month; the cumulative translation adjustment is computed, never posted. Eliminations follow the materiality threshold like any other Proposal.
