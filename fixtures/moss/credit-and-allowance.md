# Policy: credit risk and allowance for doubtful accounts
Before net terms are granted, and at every close, the guarantor behind each customer with open receivables goes through the credit cascade: identity verification, fraud score, three bureau reports, public records. Tiers: A (all scores 740+, clean) 1% reserve and 45-day terms; B (680+) 5% and 30 days; C (thin, frozen, no score) 10% and 15 days; D (below 680, discharged bankruptcy, liens, collections) 50% and prepay; BLOCK (active bankruptcy, deceased, identity failure, synthetic identity, high fraud) 100% and no terms. The allowance entry books only the change from the current reserve. Only sandbox persona data may be sent to the credit provider.
